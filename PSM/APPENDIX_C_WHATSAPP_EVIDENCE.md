## C.1 Introduction

Requirement gathering for the Quality Defect Tracking System (QDTS) began with **WhatsApp communication** with the Kak Norie / Retort Niaga manager (Nazhif). The student first obtained consent to collect business information, then asked initial questions through WhatsApp to understand business background, production process, staffing, quality checking practices, defect handling, and common defect causes. These WhatsApp messages were recorded from **9 June 2026** onwards.

After the initial WhatsApp discussions, a **structured Google Form questionnaire** was prepared and completed by Nazhif on **11 June 2026** (Appendix B). The Google Form became the main **structured** primary data source because it captured comprehensive requirement answers in a consistent format. WhatsApp in this appendix documents the **earlier initial contact** and exploratory clarification that informed the requirement gathering process, including product list, production flow, and confirmation that the most common defect at Kak Norie is wrong expiry date printing caused by failure to change the expiry date mould.

Personal phone numbers have been redacted in the screenshots where applicable to protect privacy.

## C.4 Relevance to QDTS Requirements

The WhatsApp clarifications support several QDTS design decisions:

| WhatsApp finding | QDTS feature supported |
|------------------|------------------------|
| Production flow: weigh → cook → pack → seal → retort → label → store | Nine detected stages in defect reporting |
| 3 workers; same people handle all processes | Manager + Worker role model (not full ERP) |
| Discard defect + record batch loss | Loss recording and loss reports |
| Most common defect: wrong expiry print | Expiry mismatch validation |
| Cause: forgot to change expiry mould | Root cause investigation + workflow options |
| Product list (dendeng, pes masak lemak, etc.) | Product master data and seed products |

WhatsApp in this appendix captured **initial** business context and process detail (Appendix C). The Google Form in Appendix B then collected **structured** answers on record keeping, corrective action monitoring, reporting needs, and workflow expectations in one comprehensive response. Together, WhatsApp and the Google Form support the requirement analysis in Chapters 2 and 3.
