## B.4 Relevance to QDTS Requirements

The Google Form response confirmed several requirements implemented in QDTS, including:

- replacement of paper-based defect recording with a structured digital system;
- batch and product-linked defect records, even though formal batch numbers are not always used in current practice;
- expiry date validation based on retort date and product shelf life;
- corrective action tracking and verification before case closure;
- root cause recording and analysis reports;
- loss recording and reporting;
- role-based access for Manager and Worker;
- evidence photo upload for defect cases.
